Privacy Policy – NGD (nmedea Gaming Dex)

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization and collect only what is strictly necessary to provide you with a smooth gaming and multiplayer experience:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we store your chosen username and your secure authentication credentials via Google Firebase Authentication. We do not require or collect your real name, phone number, physical address, or email address unless voluntarily provided for support.
  • Gaming & Performance Data: We track gameplay statistics, high scores, time played, active sessions, and unlocked achievements so your progress is saved securely across your devices.
  • Multiplayer & Social Data: If you use social features, your online status, friends list, and shared pack items are synchronized to enable real-time interaction with other players.
  • Device & Local Storage: Preferences such as your display theme (Dark/Light mode) and session tokens are stored locally on your device using browser local storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Maintain, secure, and authenticate your user account.
  • Synchronize and preserve your game progress and stats.
  • Power multiplayer features, friends lists, and leaderboards.
  • Monitor platform performance, fix bugs, and improve user experience.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We utilize trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers to deliver our services securely:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure account authentication and real-time database management.
  • Cloudflare / Cloudflare R2: Used for fast, reliable game asset hosting and content delivery.

Other players can view your public username, avatar, in-game stats, and online presence solely where required for multiplayer features or leaderboards.

4. Children's Privacy

NGD is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ robust security measures, including HTTPS encryption and secure database rules, to protect your data. Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You may permanently delete your account and all associated game data at any time directly through the app under Settings > Delete Account.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of NGD after changes indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, you can reach out to us via the nmedea chat platform or through our developer support portal.
