A2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you logged in.
  • To save your progress and stats.
  • To run friends, private chat, blocking, reporting and online status.
  • To review reports, enforce our Community Rules and Terms and keep players safe.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix bugs and improve performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or sale, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

A3. Who can see it

  • Everyone: nobody outside NGD can see your private messages. Other players can find your account by your exact username.
  • Your friends: can see your username, profile picture, whether you are online and which game you are playing (unless you hide your online status), and the messages you send them.
  • Players you have not accepted: cannot message you. They can only send a friend request.
  • Blocked players: cannot send you friend requests or messages in NGD.
  • Our team: may see reports and the messages included in them so we can act on them.

Private messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are protected in transit with HTTPS and stored with our database provider. We do not read private chats unless they are included in a report or we are required to by law.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the safety of players, the public or our service.

A4. Service providers

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We use these providers to run NGD, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database): accounts, progress, friends, messages, online status and reports.
  • Cloudflare (including Cloudflare R2): hosting and delivery of game files and images.

Their handling of data is covered by their own privacy policies. Data may be processed in countries other than yours.

A5. Games on NGD

NGD hosts games inside the app. Verified games published by nmedea receive your account name, ID and profile picture link and a sign-in token so you do not have to log in twice. Games not marked verified do not receive your account. Games may store their own progress for your account. Do not share personal details with other players in any game or chat.

A6. Children

NGD is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age for online services in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it. Players under 18 should use NGD with a parent or guardian’s permission where the law requires it.

A7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app at Settings > Delete account. Deleting removes your login and your game data, friends, friend requests, private chats, blocked list and online status. Messages you sent are removed from chats with your friends. Reports about other players, and the messages included in them, may be kept only for as long as needed to keep players safe and handle abuse, then deleted. Data on your device is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Backups and provider logs may take a short time to clear. See how to delete your account and data.

A8. Your choices and rights

  • Hide your online status, hide offensive words, block and report players, and decline friend requests inside NGD.
  • Delete your account and data at any time (see above).
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, South Africa under POPIA, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us. We will respond within the time the law requires.

A9. Security

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, secure authentication through Firebase, and access controls on our database. No online service is completely secure, so please choose a strong password you do not use elsewhere and never share it. If you think your account was accessed by someone else, change your password and contact us.

A10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Using NGD after a change means you accept the updated policy.

C. Delete your account and data

You can delete your NGD account and its data yourself, at any time, from inside the app. It takes less than a minute and cannot be undone.

How to delete it in the app

  1. Open NGD and log in.
  2. Tap the settings icon (gear) at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm and tap Delete forever.

What is deleted

  • Your login (username and password).
  • Your game progress and stats saved to your account.
  • Your friends list, friend requests and blocked list.
  • Your private chats and your online status.

What may be kept

Reports you made, or that were made about you, and the messages included in them, may be kept only as long as needed to keep players safe and handle abuse, then deleted. Provider backups and logs can take a short time to clear. Data stored on your device (settings and play-time statistics) is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it.

Cannot log in?

If you forgot your password or cannot open the app, contact us through , tell us your username, and we will help you delete the account. We may ask you to prove the account is yours before we delete it.
